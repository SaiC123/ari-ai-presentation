## Slide 12: Revenue, costs and sustainability

Our proposed plans start at forty-nine dollars, with usage caps to keep them affordable. Growth brings in one forty-nine monthly and costs about thirty-five dollars per customer, including our AWS allocation, leaving about one hundred fourteen before shared costs. Our six-founder pilot budgets five hundred dollars monthly for team AI tools, Vercel, a domain and contingency. About five customers at our assumed mix cover those pilot cash costs. We take no salaries initially, fund pilots ourselves and reinvest surplus. Re-check usage and production costs still need measurement.

## Slide 13: Market sizing

U.S. software publishers are a transparent market proxy, not a count of qualified SaaS customers. At our proposed annual Growth price of $1,788, the broader proxy represents $23.7 million and our starting cohort $7.6 million. Fifty paying customers would represent $89,400 ARR. This is a target, not traction.
