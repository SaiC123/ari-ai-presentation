## Slide13: Revenue, costs and sustainability

Our pilot prices start at forty-nine ninety-nine. Growth costs an estimated eighty-one dollars to deliver, leaving a modeled thirty-eight percent contribution margin before shared costs. API usage comes from our demo assumption; infrastructure and support remain estimates. During five pilots, we will log billed tokens, sandbox runtime, storage and support time for every assessment. We will expand only after observing positive contribution at the promised allowance. Our conservative plan assumes two new paid accounts monthly, plus initial pilot conversions, not forty-two. It does not break even in year one: monthly coverage requires thirty-two Founder accounts, and the model needs about four thousand dollars of funding across the year. Founders initially fund operations and reinvest receipts. These figures exclude founder pay, paid acquisition and extra usage.
