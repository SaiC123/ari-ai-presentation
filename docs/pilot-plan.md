# Proposed pilot, acquisition and expansion plan

All targets and roles below are proposals, not observed traction, signed partner commitments or new product features. Five email reports are existing feedback, not this future pilot cohort. No customer contact, assessment or attack testing was performed for this presentation edit.

## Buyer and qualification

User: founder-developer or engineering lead at a small SaaS company using AI-assisted development without dedicated security staff. Payer: the company/workspace budget owner. This is the ICP hypothesis. The growth owner checks each target for SaaS fit, team/security staffing, AI workflow, repository authority, current workaround, budget ownership and willingness to consider a scoped pilot. Record rejected and duplicate accounts. Census counts cannot fill these fields.

## Recruitment milestone and ownership

First recruitment milestone: 100 targets → 30 calls → 10 potential design partners → 5 authorized pilots → a goal of 3 total paid accounts. These are proposed targets, not achieved funnel rates or a claim that 3 of 5 pilots convert. Paid decisions can include separate direct buyers. Channels to test: founder outreach and accelerator introductions. No named accelerator relationship is asserted.

Proposed owner roles, based on the existing team lanes:
- Growth/commercial lead (Sai): target list, channel attribution, contact/call/paid ledger, quoted scope/pricing and willingness-to-pay record.
- Product lead (Vihaan): onboarding task and permission comprehension, independent task completion, assistance and feedback.
- Security/backend leads (Alan/Srihith): authorized scope, budget, assessment outcome, repair decision and independent verification artifacts.
- Intelligence/review leads (Sarosh/Sai): pinned intent/context and repair evidence.
- RL lead (Adam): simulation provenance; no real infrastructure action.

Owners review the ledger weekly. Capture week/date, anonymous account ID, channel, contact, call, qualification, invitation, approval, pilot outcome, paid decision, price shown, owner and founder minutes. Count unique accounts and cash receipts separately. Confirm individuals' acceptance of these proposed roles before operating the plan.

## Five-pilot scorecard

For each authorized scoped pilot, record repository/commit, human scope approval, provider mode, one assessment outcome (including inconclusive/error), finding usefulness and reproducibility, repair accepted/rejected with reason, independent verification result/artifact, regressions, billed API, sandbox/storage/AWS allocation, retries, repository size, support/onboarding minutes, permission comprehension and continue/pay decision. Use the existing `pilot-cost-measurement.csv` for delivery measurements. Do not use presentation test totals as security outcomes.

Planned exit gate: five complete records; scope and code-processing explanation understood; no unresolved scope violation; reproducible evidence for claims; all proposed repairs reviewed; failures retained; observed contribution positive at the promised full allowance, including realistic support allocation. A rejected fix or non-buyer is a result, not something to hide. If cost or reliability fails, narrow scope, repair reliability, cap onboarding or reprice before expanding.

## Capacity behind the 42/month scenario

The approved financial scenario adds 42 paid accounts/month from M2. This is a derived target, not supported channel throughput. ChartMogul's time-to-milestone statistic does not establish a conversion funnel.

Illustrative capacity arithmetic only: if the first milestone's target ratios (30 calls/100 contacts and 3 paid/30 calls) were reused, 42 paid would require 1,400 qualified contacts and 420 calls/month. Across six founders: about 233 contacts and 70 calls each/month. At assumed 5 minutes/contact and 30 minutes/call, that is about 327 founder-hours/month in total, or 54 hours each, before engineering, onboarding and delivery. These ratios, durations and availability are unvalidated. Introductions or self-serve acquisition could change the mix but require measured evidence; do not promise this capacity.

42 new Founder customers alone add 294 included assessments/month; retained customers compound demand. Scheduling, concurrency, support and human review must be measured on representative repositories. Growth remains a scenario until weekly channel, cost and capacity records support it. Slower intake sensitivities already exist in the model tests; the approved default remains 42.

## Roadmap exits and fallbacks

Today: engineering leads preserve a reproducible local artifact trace; remain local while full-chain transport is scripted.

Month 1: security/backend leads deliver a complete live-provider trace and five scoped pilot records, using six founders and capped API spend. If findings are unreliable, fix and repeat before adding accounts.

Months 2–3: product/growth leads record paid decisions and observed contribution at full allowance; hosted beta requires deployment/access readiness. $500 shared tools is separate from customer delivery. If trust or economics fail, narrow scope or reprice.

Months 4–6: platform leads pass permission-isolation and reliability checks before multi-repository pilots. If unmet, remain single-repository.

Enterprise, later: founders confirm controls, support capacity and adequate funding. Defer enterprise if those conditions are unmet. No hiring, certification, ARR or customer milestone is claimed as achieved.
