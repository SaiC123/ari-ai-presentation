# Research and population checks

Checked October 17, 2026. External research supports context and planning choices, not Ari customer interviews, product performance or willingness to pay. The sources below were opened during this pass; existing competitor sources are in `competitor-landscape.md`.

## Need and segment

[Veracode 2025](https://www.veracode.com/blog/genai-code-security-report/) tested over 100 models on 80 curated tasks in four languages and reported 45% of generated samples failed security tests. The selected 2025 number remains dated and scoped on slide4. A [2026 update](https://www.veracode.com/blog/spring-2026-genai-code-security/) exists; do not call the 2025 figure a current universal model failure rate.

[Check Point 2026](https://research.checkpoint.com/2026/cyber-security-report-2026/) and its [announcement](https://checkpoint.com/tw/press-releases/check-point-softwares-2026-cyber-security-report-shows-global-attacks-reach-record-levels-as-ai-accelerates-the-threat-landscape/) support 1,968 weekly attempts per organization in2025 and about70% growth since2023 in vendor telemetry. Attempts are not successful breaches. This does not establish AI coding caused the increase. The deck now removes the equals cue and labels the graph separate evidence.

[Stack Overflow 2025](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai) reports84% of survey respondents using or planning to use AI tools. The population is developers who responded, not SaaS founders without security staff. It cannot be multiplied into Census firm counts. These surveys do not validate the exact Ari ICP or its buying priority. The deck labels the profile/priorities hypotheses; Alex's clarity feedback and Samira's permissions request are specific reported observations.

## Growth and conversion

[ChartMogul2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-go-to-market-report/) analyzes2,500SaaS companies. Its B2B subscriber-milestone data supplies the approximately24-month context;42=round(1,000/24) is the user's derived linear planning target, not an observed average monthly gross-intake rate. Price point, channel, retention and survivor/sample effects matter. Citation remains directly below42 on slide13.

[First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-free-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks/) lists18.2% organic opt-in free-trial-to-paid conversion. This marketing benchmark is a proxy, not matched evidence for five assisted security pilots. Timing and2%paid churn are separate assumptions. No published evidence establishes Ari's42/month capacity. ## Pricing comparables, with distinct units

- [GitHub Code Security](https://github.com/security/plans):30USD per active committer/month; Secret Protection is a separate19USD add-on. Public-repository availability and entitlements differ. Not a price for an Ari-equivalent assessment.
- [Semgrep](https://semgrep.dev/pricing/): Free Edition has repository/contributor/AI-credit limits. Teams Code and Supply Chain each start at30USD/month/contributor; Secrets15USD. AI credit allowances and enterprise terms matter. These are module/contributor prices, not whole-workspace end-to-end assessments.
- [Snyk](https://snyk.io/plans/): Free and Team starting25USD/month, with plan test/project limits. The page explicitly excludes Evo capabilities such as AI pentesting from Free/Team, requiring Enterprise subscription. Do not compare its entry price to all platform features.

These prices support that users face alternative scopes and charging units, not a conclusion that Ari is cheaper, complete, or worth its proposed price. Retain introductory pilot prices and gather actual price/continue/pay decisions. No purchase or billing change was made.
